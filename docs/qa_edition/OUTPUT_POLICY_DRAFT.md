# Output policy — DRAFT, NON-OPERATIVE

> **DRAFT — NON-OPERATIVE.** Nothing in this document is in force. No licence is selected, no
> repository visibility changes, no CLA is adopted, no release is published. Requires owner
> approval and, where noted, qualified-counsel review. **Not legal advice.**

## The question

When you run the Bundle, who owns what comes out?

The owner's direction is deliberately user-first:

> **Bundle is the cow. The milk and calves it produces for a user belong to that user.**

In operative terms, the project should make no ownership claim merely because Bundle processed or
produced something and should grant every right the Bundle owner can grant in designated output
boilerplate. It still cannot promise title to rights the Bundle owner never held: actual ownership
and licensing remain subject to user/third-party inputs, model/provider terms, confidential or
personal data, contracts, and applicable law.

## The rule

| What it is | Proposed project position (not a legal-title determination) | Reasoning / limit |
|---|---|---|
| User-authored specifications, constraints, adapters, oracles, and test packs | **The Bundle project makes no claim merely from processing them.** | They remain attributable to their actual authors; third-party material, contracts, and law still apply. |
| User input data | **No automatic project claim.** | Data rights are not determined by this tool policy. |
| Run artifacts, metrics, reports, Pareto fronts, provenance, and derived datasets | **User-controlled to the extent rights can be held/granted.** | An artifact may still reproduce protected input, model output, personal data, or embedded source. |
| Candidates assembled only from user-supplied fragments | **User-controlled; no Bundle claim.** | The project does not determine title to the supplied fragments. |
| AI-assisted user scenarios and outputs | **No Bundle claim merely because Bundle or an inference-only assistant helped.** | Human contribution, AI-provider terms, and applicable law determine what rights exist. |
| Candidates embedding designated Bundle helper/template source | **An express output-freedom grant/exception is required.** | The Bundle owner should grant every right they can so users may use, modify, distribute, and commercialize the ordinary output. |
| The engine source itself, modified | **Proposed engine licence applies if/when activated, subject to its terms.** | This draft does not decide a particular deployment's legal obligations. |
| Output substantially embodying Bundle capability-teaching material | **User-output freedom does not authorize AI training on Bundle's own technology.** | Inference-only AI assistance is allowed; training/fine-tuning/distillation/provider-corpus ingestion is separately prohibited. |

## Required output-freedom grant

Most candidates are assembled from the user's own fragments in an order Core computes. Even so, the
owner wants an explicit output-freedom grant so users need not guess whether an ordinary output
contains a protected helper or template fragment.

Before drafting it:

1. audit representative generated candidates and source maps for copied Bundle text;
2. identify the exact helper/template paths and fragments intended to receive the exception;
3. grant users every right the Bundle owner can grant in those designated fragments for ordinary
   output use, modification, distribution, and commercialization; and
4. state that the grant does not license the engine, another Bundle installation, or protected
   third-party material.

The exception must not silently override the separate prohibition on training an AI to reproduce
Bundle's Core → Reader → Executor or higher-order composition capabilities.

## What we do not say

- We do **not** say all generated output is unconditionally proprietary to the user; no project
  policy can erase third-party rights, provider terms, privacy duties, contracts, or applicable law.
- We do **not** say ordinary output automatically inherits the Bundle engine licence.
- We propose **no automatic Bundle-project claim** over user inputs, scenarios, data, candidates,
  reports, or run results merely because the engine processed or produced them.
- User-output freedom does **not** authorize training, fine-tuning, distilling, or improving an AI
  to reproduce Bundle's own capabilities. Inference-only AI help with understanding Bundle and
  creating the user's own scenarios is permitted when no model/provider training occurs.
- The no-fee Bundle is supplied **“AS IS”**; output freedom is not a correctness, title, support,
  availability, fitness, or non-infringement warranty.

Counsel should confirm this section before publication; it is the part users read most carefully.
