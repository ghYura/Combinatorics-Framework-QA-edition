# SUCCESSION

This file exists because the author of this work lives in Kyiv, Ukraine, during
a war, and is the only person who holds the knowledge, the rights and the
access to this project. In backup terms the project's bus factor is **1**.

The purpose of this document is simple: **if the author cannot continue, this
work must not die with him.**

> ## Status: PARTIALLY IN PLACE
>
> The licence guarantee below **is** in force. The archival deposit that gives
> it something to act upon is **not yet made**.
>
> Until that deposit exists, every copy of this work sits on the author's
> machine and in his GitHub account, and a private personal repository has no
> successor mechanism. A Change Date can only open copies that exist somewhere.
>
> Read the sections below as the design, and the checklist at the end as what
> remains.

## The guarantee already built into the licence

The strongest protection is not this document — it is [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Under the Business Source License 1.1:

- Everyone who has received a copy may **copy, modify and redistribute** the
  source. That right is already granted and cannot be withdrawn from copies
  already distributed.
- On the **Change Date, 2030-08-08** — or the fourth anniversary of the first
  public distribution of a given version, whichever comes first — that version
  automatically becomes available under the **GNU AGPL v3.0-only**.

No action by the author, his heirs, a court or any custodian is required for
that conversion to happen. It is a calendar event written into the licence.

This is the answer to the bus factor. A work under "All Rights Reserved" would
have been frozen for seventy years after the author's death; this one opens on
a fixed date regardless of what happens to anyone.

## No custodians, by decision

An earlier draft of this file named three trusted people to hold mirrors. That
approach is deliberately **rejected**: a person can be pressured, deceived, or
can change their mind, and can later assert rights they were never given. A plan
that needs three individuals to remain trustworthy and reachable for years has
three soft points in it.

Survival here therefore rests on **institutions and elapsed time**, neither of
which can be persuaded.

## What actually carries the work forward

**1. The licence, already in force.** BUSL-1.1 grants every holder of a copy the
right to copy, modify and redistribute it, and that grant cannot be withdrawn
from a copy already given. On the Change Date it converts to AGPL-3.0-only
automatically. No person authorises that; it is a calendar event.

**2. An embargoed archival deposit.** A copy is deposited with a public archive
(Zenodo, operated by CERN) under an **embargo whose end date matches the
licence's Change Date**. Until then the files are closed; on that date the
archive opens them automatically. Nobody is asked, nobody can be persuaded to
withhold it, and nobody can release it early. This is what removes the "the only
copy is on the author's machine and in his account" failure — without handing
anyone a copy in the meantime.

**3. A preprint of the method.** Code can be archived; an idea has to be
published to be preserved. A public, timestamped, unalterable preprint means the
method could be rebuilt even if every copy of the source were lost.

**4. Copies the author alone controls**, in more than one physical place. These
protect against losing a machine. They deliberately do not protect against
losing the author — that is what (1)–(3) are for.

## What is deliberately NOT done

- **No custodians, no escrow agent, no trusted third party** holding a copy.
- **No GitHub organisation with a second human owner.** That is custody wearing
  a different hat.

The consequence is stated honestly: while the repositories remain private and
personal, **nobody can reach them if the author cannot** — GitHub's successor
mechanism covers public repositories only. That hole is closed by the deposit in
(2), not by giving anyone access now.

## The trade-off, stated plainly

An embargoed deposit means this work becomes public on the Change Date whether
or not the author is here. A wish that it survive him and a wish that it never
become public without his say are in direct conflict; one of them has to give.

The licence already chose. This arrangement only makes the bytes obey the same
date the licence does.
